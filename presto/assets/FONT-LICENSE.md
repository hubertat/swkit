# swkit.af font sources

`swkit.af` is generated by `tools/make_font.py` from two fonts, both under
the Apache License, Version 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0):

- **Roboto Medium**, Copyright 2011 Google Inc.
  Source: https://github.com/googlefonts/roboto-2 (`src/hinted/Roboto-Medium.ttf`)
- **Material Symbols Outlined**, Copyright Google LLC.
  Source: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons (the copy shipped in
  https://github.com/pimoroni/presto/tree/main/tools)

The glyph outlines were flattened and rescaled into PicoVector's Alright
Fonts format. No other changes were made.
